## Relevant Industry Standards or Protocols

- **ARGUS DCF Methodology** — de facto institutional standard for discounted cash flow modelling in CRE underwriting
- **NCREIF Property Index (NPI)** — benchmark index for institutional real estate performance measurement
- **MSCI Real Estate (formerly IPD)** — global benchmark for private real estate investment performance
- **IRR / NPV / Equity Multiple** — standard return metrics universally used in real estate underwriting and LP reporting
- **GIPS (Global Investment Performance Standards)** — CFA Institute standards for reporting investment performance, increasingly adopted by real estate fund managers
- **SEC Regulation D / Rule 506** — governs private placement of real estate syndication deals; affects how underwriting materials are shared with investors

## Market Research

**Market Size:** The US PropTech market is valued at approximately $25B in 2025, projected to grow to $88B by 2032 at 11.9% CAGR. Global proptech funding reached $16.7B in 2025, a 67.9% year-over-year increase and the highest level since pre-pandemic 2019. Real estate investment analysis software is a key segment of this market.

**Funding:** Altus Group (ARGUS parent) is publicly traded (AIF on TSX). Cherre raised $50M+ in venture funding. DealCheck is bootstrapped. PropStream has raised undisclosed venture funding. CoStar Group (CSGP) is the data incumbent with a market cap exceeding $30B. Organisations implementing investment management software report 30–50% reductions in manual processing time.

**Pricing Landscape:** Highly bifurcated — consumer/prosumer tools (DealCheck, Stessa, PropStream) range from free to $100/month; institutional platforms (ARGUS, Cherre, CoStar Analytics) operate on enterprise contracts typically costing tens of thousands per year. The mid-market for small syndicators and private equity firms is underserved, relying heavily on Excel models.

**Key Buyer Personas:** Institutional asset managers and REITs using ARGUS for portfolio valuation; private equity real estate funds needing deal underwriting and LP reporting; individual real estate investors and syndicators evaluating single-asset deals; commercial brokers running quick underwriting for clients; family offices building diversified real estate portfolios without institutional-grade tooling.

**Notable Trends:** AI-powered predictive analytics now improve market-timing accuracy by up to 42% for acquisitions according to industry reports; proptech funding surged to $16.7B in 2025, signalling renewed investor appetite; Cherre's Agent.STUDIO (2025) represents the first major AI-native institutional CRE analytics platform; Excel models remain dominant among small operators, representing a large displacement opportunity; integration between investment analysis tools and property management platforms (closing the acquisition-to-operations loop) is an emerging demand.

## AI-Native Opportunity

- Automated market comp analysis that pulls transaction data from CoStar, Zillow, and public records to generate underwriting benchmarks without manual research
- AI-driven scenario modelling that generates rent growth, vacancy, and cap rate assumptions calibrated to current submarket conditions rather than static analyst inputs
- Natural language deal summary generation — analysts describe a deal verbally or in notes, and the platform populates a full underwriting model with sourced assumptions
- LP reporting automation that converts raw portfolio data into investor-ready quarterly reports with narrative commentary and performance attribution analysis
- Acquisition opportunity scoring that continuously scans listed and off-market properties against a fund's predefined investment criteria, ranking deals by fit and flagging time-sensitive opportunities
